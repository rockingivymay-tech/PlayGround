    How to cook

    1. 1
      Chop. Send lettuce, tomato or onion to the board. Tap the board (or swipe across it) until it is bite-sized.
    2. 2
      Sear. Send a patty to the skillet. Choose a heat, flip it once, and take it off when the meter is in the green.
    3. 3
      Stack & serve. Send the finished pieces to the plate. When the order is complete, serve it with a smile.

    There are no timers. Customers wait happily forever. Nothing can be failed. If something burns, toss it and begin again.
